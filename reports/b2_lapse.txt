SELECTED MODEL: random forest (test F1 0.655, recall 0.652, precision 0.659)
Justification: missing a customer who is about to lapse (a false negative) loses all of that customer's future revenue and a win-back later is
far costlier than prevention, whereas wrongly targeting a loyal customer (a false positive) only costs a small coupon or message; so recall on the
lapsed class matters at least as much as precision, and F1 balances the two. The random forest has the higher test F1 and catches
65% of lapsing customers at 66% precision, well above the 0 recall of the do-nothing baseline.
Consistency check: cross-validation on the training rows ranks the models the same way (random forest has the higher CV F1: RF 0.681 vs LR 0.651), so the choice does not rest on the test set alone.
