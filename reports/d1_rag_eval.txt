TF (term frequency) measures how often a word occurs inside one chunk, i.e. how much the chunk is about it; IDF (inverse document frequency) measures how rare the word
is across all chunks (log of chunks / chunks containing it), so common words such as 'the' or 'order' count little and distinctive ones such as 'escalate' or 'rain' count a lot.
Limitation: TF-IDF matches words, not meaning. 'reimbursement tardy parcel' means a late-delivery refund, but its words shared with the policy vocabulary: none; so every score is [0.0, 0.0, 0.0] and the 'top' chunks are just the first three in file order (['delivery_policy.txt', 'delivery_policy.txt', 'delivery_policy.txt']).
