WHEN IS FEW-SHOT WORTH THE EXTRA TOKENS?
The few-shot prompt costs 160.5 tokens per classified ticket against 63.9 for zero-shot (+96.6, about 151% more) and in return accuracy rises from 85.7% to 100.0%, i.e. all 21 of the
zero-shot mistakes disappear. The mistakes are not random: zero-shot sends every fraud and technical ticket that mentions a charge or payment to billing, and a
mis-filed fraud ticket misses the 2-hour security SLA, so for the high-stakes categories 97 extra tokens per ticket is cheap insurance. When tickets are
short, the categories are unambiguous and a wrong label is cheap to fix, the cheaper zero-shot prompt is enough.
Honest caveat: the mock switches its keyword priority when the system prompt contains 'EXAMPLES:', so the 100% measures this mock's behaviour,
not in-context learning; with a real model the gain must be re-measured on held-out tickets.
